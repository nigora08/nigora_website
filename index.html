Selected work

Current software, then the hardware and teaching projects that came before it.

  1. 01 2026

    Bulldogs Build

    A fabrication planner for students using Yale shops. You type a project and a few constraints — budget, size, material, experience, precision — and it ranks a route: machine, training, materials, and a build sequence. Equipment facts are tied to public CEID, Wright Lab, YSoA, and YINQE pages. It is not an official Yale system.

    Repository

  2. 02 2026

    Evaluation recognition

    A manual replication of Needham et al. (2025), Large Language Models Often Know When They Are Being Evaluated. I sampled evaluation and deployment transcripts, reformatted the option lists, and recorded chatbot answers by hand. Twenty-five responses are in the snapshot; collection is still open, and the counts are split by model and reasoning setting.

    Repository Paper

  3. 03 2024

    Tensegrity robot

    Summer research at the Institute of Smart Systems and Artificial Intelligence, Nazarbayev University, in Astana. A tensegrity is a network of rigid struts and soft cables; it changes shape by changing its internal tension. I built and programmed one, and compared linear actuators with the lab’s earlier actuation to see how the motion should be controlled.

    ISSAI · Astana

  4. 04 2023–24

    A short book on special relativity

    Half a year of writing a small book that explains special relativity to teenagers, without assuming a physics class. More than fifty people read the first version. The project was a finalist in the 2024 Rise Challenge. The next draft is general relativity.

    Rise Challenge finalist

  5. 05 2023

    Fractions Flora

    A physical learning aid for dividing fractions, made for a sixth grader who was stuck on the operation. Flowers and leaves are split into parts you can put together. It sits where math and object design meet.

    Classroom object

Record

  1. 2026 —

    Yale College

  2. 2024 —

    ISSAI, Nazarbayev University

    Four-week summer research program in Astana, five days a week. Selected in the top tenth of applicants. Alongside the tensegrity robot, I gathered background for Dr. Zhanat Makhataeva’s paper on virtual conversational agents in augmented reality and how they affect cognition.

  3. 2022–23 —

    Robotics club, Tashkent International School

    Teaching assistant for a club of more than ten students. I already knew EV3, so I asked for the assistant role instead of repeating the beginner track. Day to day that meant construction, programs, and missing parts. I also started a sumo-robot project and ran it through to a showcase for the school.

  4. 2022 —

    Biomedical equipment, Dushanbe

    Intern at the Tajik National Institute of Gynecology, Obstetrics, and Perinatal Medicine, supported by GIZ. I assisted repairs on an operating table, an X-ray, and an endoscope, and learned a multimeter and a soldering iron on devices that were actually in use.

  5. 2020–21 —

    STEM writing

    An Instagram journal for girls in Central Asia, and for parents deciding whether engineering and programming were open to their daughters. Posts mixed the case for STEM with work I was doing myself.
